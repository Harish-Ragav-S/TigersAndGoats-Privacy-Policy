Privacy Policy for Tigers & Goats

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

Swag Studios Games ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Tigers & Goats handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Tigers & Goats does not require users to create an account, register, or log in.

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or account credentials through the game.

2. Local Data Storage

The game may store gameplay-related information and settings locally on your device. This may include game progression, difficulty unlocks, and other game preferences required for the game to function properly.

This locally stored information is not transmitted by the current version of the game to our servers.

Gameplay-related information stored locally remains on the user's device until it is cleared by the user, the app's data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

3. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Tigers & Goats does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs and does not use them to collect information from users.

Swag Studios Games may introduce advertising or analytics services in future versions. If our data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before or alongside the release of the applicable version.

4. Device Vibration

The current version of Tigers & Goats may use the device's vibration function for gameplay feedback.

The vibration permission is not used to collect or transmit personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of Tigers & Goats does not use third-party services to collect or transmit personal information.

This may change in future versions if additional services, such as advertising or analytics providers, are integrated. Any applicable privacy disclosures will be updated when such services are introduced.

6. Children's Privacy

Tigers & Goats does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the current version does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal information, users can play the game without providing personal information to us.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. The current version does not transmit the locally stored gameplay information to our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this location with a revised effective date.
